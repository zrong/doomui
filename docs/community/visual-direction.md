# Quiet Transfer

Two planes and one deliberate arrow express a controlled transfer. Repeated rectangular forms give the composition a shared grammar from the smallest icon to the cover. Precise alignment and measured spacing carry the idea without borrowed product imagery.

An off-white ground gives the forms room to breathe; deep green provides structure, while a single pale lime accent marks the managed visual layer. Colour is used sparingly and consistently, with careful attention to contrast and small-size readability.

Typography pairs a broad, confident product name with compact monospaced process labels. The cover remains a workflow diagram, not a product screenshot or a claim of host acceptance. Every label has a clear position and a generous margin.

Craft is expressed through consistent corner radii, balanced panel weights and a crisp arrow at icon scale. The image is original geometric artwork and contains no private design, runtime screenshot, pairing information or third-party product logo.
